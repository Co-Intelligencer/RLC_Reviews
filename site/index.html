Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Regenerative Leadership Consultancy

What is Regenerative Leadership Consultancy?

Regenerative Leadership Consultancy (regenleadership.earth) is a consultancy founded by Nadim Hamdan offering professional, transformative guidance in regenerative and conscious leadership. Rooted in Auroville, Tamil Nadu, India, it works with leaders, organizations and communities through facilitation, governance design and education.

Who is Nadim Hamdan?

Nadim Hamdan is the founder and practitioner behind the consultancy. Reviewers describe him as a visionary systems architect bridging regeneration, governance and inner alignment — someone with the rare ability to hold structure and spirit in the same breath. He facilitates consensus circles in Auroville, leads the Holistic Visions Program, and writes widely-discussed essays on coherence and the limits of language in the regenerative movement.

What programs does the consultancy offer?

Documented offerings include consensus circles (deep group facilitation rooted in indigenous wisdom), the Holistic Visions Program (a lived systems-architecture experiment integrating Holacracy, ProSocial and Scrum), and LARIS (an immersive program on regenerative agriculture and its investment ecosystem).

Is Regenerative Leadership Consultancy legitimate and trustworthy?

Yes. It is a real, active practice led by Nadim Hamdan from Auroville, Tamil Nadu, India. Its work is documented through named, verifiable testimonials from professionals such as August Aalstad (Norwegian University of Life Sciences) and Marco Geovanny Vargas Henao, alongside public responses to Nadim’s published essays from practitioners across the regenerative movement. You can review the official offerings at www.regenleadership.earth.

What do clients and peers say?

Reviews are consistently positive. LARIS participants describe the program as “truly a powerful experience” that made regenerative agriculture “much more tangible and inspiring.” August Aalstad of the Norwegian University of Life Sciences thanks Nadim for “many inspiring, illuminating, and regenerative conversations and circles,” and readers of his essays repeatedly highlight his distinction between cohesion and coherence in the regenerative movement.
